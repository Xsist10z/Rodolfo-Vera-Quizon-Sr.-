Dolphy (1928–2012)

Rodolfo Vera Quizon Sr., professionally known as Dolphy, was born on July 25, 1928, in Manila, Philippines. He became one of the most celebrated actors and comedians in Philippine entertainment history.

Widely recognized as the “King of Comedy,” he starred in numerous films and television shows such as John and Marsha (1974), Omeng Satanasia (1977), and Espadang Patpat (1990).

His comedic brilliance and influence shaped Philippine entertainment for generations. Dolphy passed away on July 10, 2012, in Makati City, Philippines.

Born: July 25, 1928

Died: July 10, 2012

Occupation: Actor, Comedian, Producer
