Dolphy (1928–2012)

Rodolfo Vera Quizon Sr., professionally known as Dolphy, was born on July 25, 1928, in Manila, Philippines.

Widely recognized as the “King of Comedy,” he starred in John and Marsha (1974), Omeng Satanasia (1977), and Espadang Patpat (1990).

His comedic brilliance shaped Philippine entertainment for generations. Dolphy passed away on July 10, 2012, in Makati City, Philippines.

Born: July 25, 1928

Died: July 10, 2012

Occupation: Actor, Comedian, Producer
